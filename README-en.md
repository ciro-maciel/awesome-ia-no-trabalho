- [Awesome IA no escritório](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-awesome-ia-no-escritorio) - AI for secretaries, assistants and administrative aides: assistants, privacy, official writing, and scams arriving by email. `free`

- [Awesome IA no primeiro trabalho](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-awesome-ia-no-primeiro-trabalho) - AI for people starting to work: assistants, privacy, where to look for jobs, first-job rights, and fake job scams. `free`

## Privacy in AI

Where to turn off the use of your conversations to train the model.

- [Anthropic: onde mudar o treino](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-anthropic-onde-mudar-o-treino) - How to stop your conversations with Claude from being used to train the model. `official`
- [Google: atividade do Gemini](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-google-atividade-do-gemini) - How to turn off Gemini's activity history and what changes with it. `official`
- [Microsoft: controles de privacidade do Copilot](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-microsoft-controles-de-privacidad) - Where to find the privacy and training controls for Copilot. `official`
- [OpenAI: controles de dados](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-openai-controles-de-dados) - How to stop conversations on ChatGPT from being used to train the model. `official`
- [WhatsApp: privacidade](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-whatsapp-privacidade) - What WhatsApp says about privacy, including when you talk to Meta AI. `official`

## Data protection

The law and the authority that apply to people's data.

- [LGPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-lgpd) - The General Data Protection Law, in the compiled text from Planalto. `official` `free`
- [ANPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-anpd) - The National Data Protection Authority, with guides and regulations on the LGPD. `official` `free`

## Security and scams

To recognize a scam before falling for it.

- [Cartilha de Segurança do CERT.br](https://go.mymarketing.click/iatrab-cartilha-de-seguranca-do-cert-br) - The CERT.br internet security guide, with booklets on scams, passwords and phones. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from whoever sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid by usage model, API and SDK by how you integrate. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
